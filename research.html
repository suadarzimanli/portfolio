Urban forestry · Municipal SR research · Open data

Zürich Trees (municipal SR research)

A design study and city-wide analysis: resident reports anchored to one tree in the City of Zürich open Baumkataster, kept as one service request from intake to closure, with public tracking that never shows who reported.

81,403 trees analysed Public tracking DOI · Zenodo

Research focus: How much location ambiguity tree-anchored map intake removes compared with address-only reports, and which map-selection rule stays reliable when taps are imprecise.

GEOINT · Forecasting · ASP.NET Core

GEOINT Crisis Zones

A web GIS for 7,794 ACLED protest and political violence events in Poland (2020–2024) — overview to single event, on desktop and phone — with a transparent county forecast and an evaluation of eight forecasting methods.

7,794 events 8 methods compared DOI · Zenodo

Research focus: Whether a transparent moving-average forecast holds up against learned models for sparse county-month event counts — and how much the evaluation design itself changes the answer.

Infrastructure GIS · Operator workflow · ML

Water Leak Monitoring Dashboard

A teaching dashboard and simulation study on leak alarms: with every detector held to the same rate of wasted crew trips, a context check using only what an operator knows lifts leaks caught from 83–90% to 97% and leak water saved from 86–88% to 93%.

40 simulated networks 93% leak water saved Paper + code public

Research focus: Comparing leak detectors at the same false-alert budget by leaks caught and water saved, and showing that what an operator knows — the schedule and the weather — matters more than the choice of detector.

Remote sensing · Grid analysis · Leaflet

Urban Heat Island — Łódź

Sentinel-2 NDVI and Landsat-8 LST combined on a 500 m grid across Łódź — with regression, classified rasters, and an open web map for neighbourhood-level exploration.

1,544 grid cells R² = 0.132 GEE pipeline

Research focus: How much vegetation explains where it is hot at grid scale — and whether a weak city-wide correlation still matters when heat and greenness vary block by block.
